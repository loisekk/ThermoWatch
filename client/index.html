EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

A GIS decision-support platform,
not just another fire map.

FIRMS detects thermal anomalies. STA performs a binary vegetation / non-vegetation split. ThermoWatch performs 10-way classification of the anomaly, identifies the specific facility and hazard class, and distinguishes a new incident from a standing regulatory problem.

10×

Multi-class industrial classification

8 industrial subtypes — refinery, steel, gas flare, cement, smelter, waste incineration, power plant, chemical — plus unknown_industrial and natural_fire. Probability-averaged RF + HistGradientBoosting ensemble.

Industrial context fusion

FIRMS thermal detections correlated with OpenStreetMap facility records, NBC 2016 hazard classes (G-I / G-II / G-III) and ESA WorldCover land-cover proxies via Haversine nearest-facility matching.

Persistence intelligence

A five-day static-thermal-anomaly (STA) threshold separates transient fires from chronic thermal sources — the difference between an incident response and a regulatory follow-up.

Positioning. FIRMS detects thermal anomalies; STA separates vegetation from non-vegetation. ThermoWatch identifies which of eight industrial subtypes is burning, names the facility, and states whether the event is a new incident or a standing thermal source requiring regulatory attention.

CAPABILITIES

Four engines, one open-data pipeline.

Every capability below is live in the dashboard further down this page — click through and operate it.

⌁Thermal ingestion & feature engineering

MODIS (1 km) + VIIRS SNPP (375 m) NRT polling with rate-limit aware retries and exponential backoff. A fixed 12-feature contract: frp, brightness_k, night_ratio, diurnal_variance, persist_days, facility_distance_km, facility_hazard_enc, forest_proxy, agri_window, cluster_density, detections_30d, frp_stability.

⌗ML inference with graceful precedence

① Trained bundle (RF + HistGB ensemble, optional XGBoost) → ② optional ST-GNN spatio-temporal head (PyG) → ③ heuristic ensemble, always available. model_provenance() reports which path served every prediction.

◎Persistence & chronic-source tracking

Consecutive detection-day counting per 0.1° cell. Crossing the 5-day STA threshold emits persistence:detected over WebSocket and promotes the cell to the Chronic Thermal Sources board.

⛨Response recommendation engine

Nearest fire stations with ETA, vehicle & personnel totals, water volume, evacuation radius by NBC hazard class, staging point and wind bearing — decision support, never automated response.

    MODEL CARD

    Metrics, provenance and limitations — stated honestly.

    0.000macro-F1 (headline)
    0.000weighted-F1
    0inference p50
    0samples · stratified

    Ensemble

    tw-ensemble-v1 — probability-averaged RandomForest + HistGradientBoostingClassifier (+ optional XGBoost), class_weight='balanced_subsample', stratified 70/15/15 split (2800/600/600).

    Provenance

    model_provenance() reports the serving path per prediction: ① trained bundle → ② ST-GNN head (PyG, optional) → ③ heuristic ensemble. Eval metrics travel with every response.

    Why macro-F1?

    Industrial fires are rare. Macro-F1 (not accuracy) is the headline metric, with balanced weighting, stratified splits and per-class precision/recall/F1 reported in full.

    classprecisionrecallf1support

    rows = true label · columns = predicted · hover a cell for exact counts · test split n=600 (60/class)
